1. Introduction

MCQ Winner respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when using the MCQ Winner educational service.

2. Information We May Collect

When creating or using an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • School information provided by the user
  • A/L stream and examination year
  • MCQ results and scores
  • Subscription status

3. How We Use Information

The information collected may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide MCQ and educational services
  • Save and display MCQ results
  • Provide ranking features
  • Manage subscriptions and access
  • Communicate important service-related information
  • Improve the service and user experience

4. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

MCQ Winner does not intentionally store complete payment card details such as full card numbers, CVV numbers, or similar sensitive payment credentials on its own website.

Payment information is handled by the relevant payment service provider according to its own privacy and security practices.

5. Firebase and Data Storage

MCQ Winner uses Firebase services for certain application functions, including authentication and database services.

Information required for the operation of the service may be stored securely through these services.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when it is necessary to provide the service, process payments, maintain technical infrastructure, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Ranking Information

MCQ Winner may display limited information such as a user's name, score, and ranking position as part of the ranking feature.

Sensitive personal information such as passwords and payment credentials will not be publicly displayed.

8. Account Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security against every possible technical threat.

Users should also keep their passwords and account credentials confidential.

9. Children's and Students' Privacy

MCQ Winner is an educational service intended for students. We encourage students to provide only information that is necessary for using the service and to avoid sharing unnecessary personal information publicly.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or application may use browser storage or similar technologies where necessary to maintain login sessions and provide application functionality.

11. Third-Party Services

MCQ Winner may rely on third-party services such as Firebase and payment service providers. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated "Last Updated" date.
