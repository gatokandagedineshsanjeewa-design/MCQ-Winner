1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances in which a refund may be requested for MCQ Winner subscription services.

MCQ Winner provides educational MCQ practice services for A/L students, including Chemistry and Physics MCQ practice.

2. Subscription Payment

The current subscription price is Rs.100 per week.

Payments may be processed through a third-party payment service provider.

3. Refund Requests

Users who believe they are eligible for a refund may contact MCQ Winner using the contact details provided on this website.

Refund requests may be reviewed based on the circumstances of the payment and the subscription service.

4. Refund Approval

Refunds are not automatically guaranteed for every payment. Each refund request will be reviewed before a refund is approved.

🔄 Important Refund Information

If a refund is approved, the refund will be made to the same payment method / media through which the payment was initiated.

5. Payment Processing

Where a payment has been processed through a third-party payment service provider, the refund process may also be subject to the payment provider's applicable processing procedures.

MCQ Winner does not intentionally store complete payment card details such as full card numbers or CVV numbers on its own website.

6. Incorrect or Duplicate Payments

If a user believes that a payment was made incorrectly or that a duplicate payment occurred, the user should contact MCQ Winner as soon as possible with the relevant payment information.

The request will be reviewed and, where applicable, handled according to this Refund Policy.

7. Subscription Access

Refund approval may affect the user's subscription access depending on the circumstances of the refund.

8. Changes to This Refund Policy

MCQ Winner may update this Refund Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated "Last Updated" date.
